## Detailed description

```
Learn vocabulary from what you actually read — and keep it with fun, bite-sized lessons.

WordHarvest turns every web page into a language lesson. Double-click a word you don't know and a small popup translates it right next to the word. Save it, and WordHarvest keeps the sentence you found it in. Then short, game-like lessons help you remember it for good.

HARVEST WORDS WHILE YOU READ
• Double-click any word on any website to see its translation.
• Click Save (or turn on auto-save). Every new word earns XP.
• The sentence, page title and link are saved with the word. Found it again elsewhere? The new sentence is added to the same word.

LEARN WITH GAME-LIKE LESSONS
• Exercises that grow with you: meet the word, pick its meaning, listen, fill in the blank in your own sentence, type the missing word, match the pairs.
• Answers are checked for you — small typos and missing accents still count.
• Mistakes come back at the end of the lesson so you get them right.
• Smart scheduling (spaced repetition) brings each word back right before you'd forget it.

STAY MOTIVATED
• XP, levels and a daily goal you choose.
• Daily streak with a weekly calendar, and streak freezes that save your streak on a missed day.
• Achievements, combo bonuses, sounds and confetti.
• Word strength bars show what you've mastered.

MORE
• Private by default: uses Chrome's built-in on-device translator when available, with an optional free online fallback (MyMemory) — or turn it off.
• Translate into English or 35+ other languages, with automatic language detection.
• Browse, search and edit your words; export to JSON, CSV or Anki.
• No account, no ads, no tracking. Your words and progress stay in your browser.

Open source (MIT): https://github.com/talipturkmen/wordharvest
```
